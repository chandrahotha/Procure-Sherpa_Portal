# Getting Started

## 1. Open the app

Go to [procuresherpa.vercel.app](https://procuresherpa.vercel.app). There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. It opens straight into the Command Center.

## 2. Pick a starting point

From the Command Center you can go in either direction, depending on what you need right now:

- **New to procurement, or want a structured curriculum?** Open the **Procurement Academy**. It walks through the full procurement lifecycle, spend analysis, sourcing, negotiation, contracting, and supplier management, with knowledge checks along the way.
- **Need to get something done today?** Open the **Operations Hub**. It holds the calculators, standard operating procedures, checklists, and templates you'd reach for on an active purchase.

## 3. Try a calculator

A good first stop is the **Compare Quotes** or **Total Cost of Ownership** calculator. Enter a few numbers and the result updates instantly. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you close the tab and come back later, anything you saved is still there, stored only in that browser.

## 4. Save your work

Any calculation, checklist, or note can be saved to your workspace. Saved items live in your browser's local storage on that device. There is a Backup / Restore page if you want to export your workspace as a file, for example to move it to another computer.

## 5. Use it offline

Once the page has loaded once, it keeps working without an internet connection. That makes it usable on a flight, in a facility with no signal, or anywhere else connectivity is not guaranteed.
