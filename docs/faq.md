# Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it really free?**
Yes. The full toolkit, the Academy curriculum, and every calculator are free to use, every time you open it. There is no paid tier and no trial period.

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. There is no sign-up and no login. You open the link and start using it.

**Where does my data go?**
Nowhere but your own device. Everything you enter, calculate, or save stays in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, tracked, or shared.

**Does it work without an internet connection?**
Yes, once the page has loaded once. It is built to keep working offline.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes. It works in any modern browser, desktop or mobile.

**Is it a replacement for our ERP or procurement system?**
No. Procure Sherpa is a learning and decision-support tool: calculators, SOPs, checklists, and training content. It is not an ERP, and it doesn't replace your organization's actual purchasing, approval, or financial systems.

**Can I trust the numbers it produces?**
The calculators are built around real, documented procurement methods (total cost of ownership, landed cost, negotiation savings, and so on). That said, approval thresholds, tax treatment, and legal requirements vary by organization and location, so always verify against your own company's policy before relying on a result operationally.

**Does it issue certificates or qualifications?**
No. Academy progress and exam scores are tracked so you can see your own learning history, but Procure Sherpa is not an accredited body and doesn't claim to issue procurement credentials.

**How do I back up what I've saved?**
There's a Backup / Restore option inside the app that exports your saved workspace as a file, so you can keep a copy or move it to another device.
