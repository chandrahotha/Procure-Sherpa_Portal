# Privacy, in plain language

**The short version: your data stays on your device. We never see it.**

## What we collect

Nothing. Procure Sherpa doesn't have a server component that receives or stores your information. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, and no account system, so there is nothing tied to your identity to collect in the first place.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter, calculators, saved checklists, templates, and workspace notes, is stored locally in your browser, on your own device. It stays there until you clear it, export it, or clear your browser's site data.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, by default. The app makes no outbound network requests once it has loaded. If you choose to export a backup file or copy a result to send to a colleague, that's an action you take deliberately; the app itself never sends anything on its own.

## Cookies and tracking

None. There are no cookies, no third-party scripts, and no telemetry of any kind.

## If you use a shared or work computer

Because saved data lives in that browser's local storage, anyone with access to the same browser profile on the same device could see what you've saved there. Use the Backup / Restore feature to export sensitive work if you need to clear it from a shared machine.
